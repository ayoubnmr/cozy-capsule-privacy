Privacy Policy for Cozy Capsule

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cozy Capsule, developed by Ayoub Nmr, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Process

Cozy Capsule does not require an account. The app does not intentionally send custom name, email address, phone number, contacts, exact location, health data, or free-form user content to its analytics or crash-reporting services. Google services may still process technical, device, usage, diagnostic, and related information as described below and in Google's own policies.

Depending on your region and applicable consent settings, collection and use of certain analytics or advertising data may be limited or adjusted. Analytics and advertising consent are handled separately where required; an advertising choice does not automatically control all analytics processing.

2. Local Game Data

Core game progress is stored on your device. Cozy Capsule does not currently offer an account-based save or its own cloud-save service. Locally stored game state may include:

  • Collectible ownership and collection progress
  • Restoration and Living World/Atrium progress
  • Shards, duplicate and capsule progression, and pity progress
  • Daily capsule, Daily Cozy List, and Daily Bonus progress
  • Mastery and first-session progress
  • Game preferences, including sound and haptics settings

This state remains until app data is cleared or the app is removed, subject to any backup or restore behavior configured through your device or operating-system account. Gameplay events described below may still be sent to Google services.

3. Analytics

Cozy Capsule uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate app usage, gameplay progression, and feature engagement and to improve the app. Custom events may describe capsule interactions, restoration, Living World/Atrium activity, Daily Cozy List activity, collection and mastery progress, duplicate and shard interactions, Collector Capsule activity, and rewarded-ad lifecycle events. These events are designed not to include the direct personal details listed above.

Google may process technical and device information, app usage, identifiers, and event data under its applicable service settings and policies. See Firebase Analytics documentation and Firebase privacy and security information.

4. Crash Reporting

Google Firebase Crashlytics helps diagnose crashes and non-fatal errors and improve app reliability. Reports may contain diagnostic and technical information such as app/build version, device or operating-system details, stack traces, and app-set gameplay context such as the current area, collectible ID, progression, restoration, or Atrium state. Crash reports are not guaranteed to be free of device or technical information.

Learn more in the Crashlytics documentation.

5. Advertising

Cozy Capsule currently has one optional rewarded-ad opportunity: the Daily Bonus. You choose whether to watch an ad. If you earn the reward, the app grants +10 existing shards, at most once per local calendar day. Declining or being unable to watch does not block core gameplay. The current v1 implementation has no banner, interstitial, or app-open placement.

Cozy Capsule uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for this placement. Depending on your consent, region, device settings, and available identifiers, Google advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP/network information, app and ad interactions, consent or privacy choices, and ad measurement or diagnostic data. Google's handling is governed by its own policies and settings. This description does not promise a particular advertising-personalization outcome.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

Cozy Capsule uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and AdMob Privacy & Messaging where applicable to request consent, manage privacy choices, and determine whether ads may be requested. In regions where a message or consent form is required, UMP may show it. When UMP indicates that privacy options are required, the app exposes a Privacy choices option in Settings so you can revisit those choices.

You can also use available operating-system and account settings to manage certain device identifiers, permissions, or ad preferences. Available controls depend on your device, account, and region.

7. Third-Party Services

Cozy Capsule uses these Google services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Information processed by these services is handled under Google's applicable terms, privacy notices, and service settings. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase privacy and security
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services
  • Google UMP documentation

8. Data Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you clear Cozy Capsule's app data or remove the app, subject to device backup and restore settings. Google services retain information according to their own policies and the retention settings configured for those services. Cozy Capsule does not set or promise a single retention period for third-party data.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the app. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children and Target Audience

Cozy Capsule is not specifically directed to children unless indicated otherwise by the applicable store listing. The app's audience and advertising treatment may also depend on the store settings, your region, and applicable consent choices.

11. Your Choices

  • Watching the Daily Bonus rewarded ad is optional.
  • Declining an ad does not block the core game.
  • Privacy choices are available in the app when UMP indicates they are required.
  • You may use available device or account controls for certain permissions and identifiers.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may change if the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
