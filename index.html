Privacy Policy for Cozy Capsule

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cozy Capsule, developed by Ayoub Nmr, handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Cozy Capsule does not require an account and does not directly ask users to provide personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone data, or payment information.

The app uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Through this advertising service, certain technical and usage data may be collected or processed, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • App interactions, such as ad-related and in-app interaction events
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available

2. Local Game Data

Cozy Capsule stores gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress can be restored between sessions.

This may include:

  • Collected items and collection progress
  • Duplicate-item shards
  • Daily capsule state
  • Game preferences and settings

This gameplay progress is stored locally on the device and is not sent to our own servers.

3. Advertising

Cozy Capsule uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers. This information may be used for:

  • Advertising or marketing
  • Ad measurement and analytics
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance

Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Cozy Capsule uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required.

Users in regions where consent is required may be shown a privacy or consent form before advertising data is processed for certain purposes.

Where supported, users can review or change applicable advertising privacy choices from the Privacy Choices option in Cozy Capsule settings.

5. Analytics

Cozy Capsule does not currently use a separate first-party analytics SDK.

However, Google Mobile Ads may process app interaction and diagnostic information for advertising analytics, ad measurement, performance, and fraud prevention.

6. Third-Party Services

Cozy Capsule uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent management.

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy and data-handling policies. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

7. Permissions and Device Identifiers

Cozy Capsule does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Precise location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media storage
  • Phone or call information

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may use the Android Advertising ID where available for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress and settings stored locally on your device generally remain until you:

  • Clear Cozy Capsule's app storage/data through Android settings; or
  • Uninstall Cozy Capsule.

Cozy Capsule does not provide user accounts and does not maintain a server-side account or personal gameplay record for users.

Advertising-related data processed by Google is handled according to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies.

9. Data Security

Cozy Capsule is designed to minimize direct data collection by the developer. Local gameplay data remains on the device.

Data transmitted by third-party advertising services is handled using the security measures provided by those services, including encrypted transmission where applicable.

10. Children's Privacy

Cozy Capsule does not provide account registration, social features, user-generated content, or mechanisms for directly submitting personal information to the developer.

Advertising behavior may vary depending on the user's region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Cozy Capsule changes its features, advertising setup, or data-handling practices.

Any updated policy will show a revised "Last updated" date.
